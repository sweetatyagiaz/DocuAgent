Acme Corp Employee Handbook

Section 1: Refund Policy
Acme Corp offers a 30-day money-back guarantee on all software subscriptions.
Customers must request a refund through the support portal within 30 days of
purchase. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days and are issued to
the original payment method. Annual plans are refunded on a pro-rated basis
after the first 30 days; monthly plans are not eligible for partial refunds
after the 30-day window.

Section 2: Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week without prior manager
approval. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from both the
employee's manager and HR. All remote employees must be reachable during
core hours, 10 AM to 3 PM in their local time zone.

Section 3: Vacation and Time Off
Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid time off (PTO) per year during
their first two years, increasing to 20 days starting in year three.
Unused PTO up to 5 days may be carried over into the next calendar year.
PTO requests should be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance through the
HR portal.

Section 4: Expense Reimbursement
Employees may submit expense reports for business-related travel, client
meals, and approved software subscriptions. Reimbursement requests must be
submitted within 60 days of the expense with an itemized receipt attached.
Expenses over $500 require pre-approval from a department head.

Section 5: Security Policy
All employees must enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on their company
accounts. Laptops must be encrypted and password-protected. Any suspected
security incident must be reported to the IT Security team within 24 hours
of discovery.
